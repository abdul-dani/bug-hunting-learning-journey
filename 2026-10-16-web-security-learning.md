2. Google Dorking

What I learned

Google dorking uses advanced search operators to refine search queries and locate specific information indexed by Google.

Examples:

- "site:example.com" — restrict results to a particular domain.
- "filetype:pdf site:example.com" — search for indexed PDF files on a domain.
- "intitle:"index of" site:example.com" — search for pages with matching titles.

Use these techniques for legitimate research, public information discovery, and authorized security assessments. Publicly indexed information is not automatically authorized for access, use, or exploitation.
