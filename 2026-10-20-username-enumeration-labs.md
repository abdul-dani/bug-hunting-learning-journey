Practical Bug Hunting: Username Enumeration

As part of my ongoing bug hunting development, I completed two labs on PortSwigger Web Security Academy focused on username enumeration in password-based login systems.

The labs covered:

- Username enumeration via different responses: Identifying valid usernames through noticeably different application responses.
- Username enumeration via subtly different responses: Investigating less obvious response differences that may reveal whether a username exists.

This practice helped me understand how seemingly minor differences in login behaviour can expose information that could support subsequent password-based attacks.

Key takeaway: Secure authentication requires careful error handling and appropriate protections against automated login attempts.

I'm continuing to build my practical skills through structured labs, hands-on testing, and documenting what I learn along the way.
